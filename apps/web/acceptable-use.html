What changed in this version

This version replaces the version of November 8, 2026. It adds rules for share links, branding, service tokens and sensitive data, says what a notice about illegal content should contain and what we tell you when we restrict something, and explains that a person, not an automated system, decides what we restrict.

Content you may not publish

Do not publish, link to or share through Portifact anything that:

  • tries to trick people into giving passwords, payment details or other sensitive information (phishing), or imitates the sign-in pages of Portifact or anyone else;
  • contains malware, exploits or code that harms visitors' devices or data, or mines cryptocurrency in their browsers;
  • is sexually explicit, or sexualizes minors in any way. Child sexual abuse material is reported to the authorities;
  • infringes copyrights, trademarks or other rights, including pirated software, media or other files;
  • impersonates a person, company or organization, or misleads people about who is behind it;
  • threatens, harasses or incites violence against people or groups, or promotes terrorism or violent extremism;
  • shares other people's private information without a lawful reason, such as their contact details, documents or credentials;
  • is spam, including pages made only to promote links or manipulate search engines;
  • is otherwise illegal where you live or in the United States.

Things you may not do

  • Use several accounts or other tricks to get around quotas, rate limits, the wait before new accounts can make artifacts public, a suspension or a ban.
  • Use Portifact as a general file host or content delivery network, for example to distribute large downloads or serve assets for other sites.
  • Attack, overload or disrupt Portifact, or try to reach artifacts, accounts or data you were not given access to. Good-faith security research that follows our security policy is welcome.
  • Write comments, review notes or content meant to make someone else's agent take harmful actions (prompt injection), or to collect data from the people who open an artifact without telling them.
  • Send automated requests at a volume that harms the service or other users.
  • Resell or rebrand the hosted service as your own.

Data that needs special protection

Portifact is not designed for data that needs special legal protection. Do not store any of the following on Portifact without a written agreement with us: health records covered by laws such as HIPAA, payment card data, government identifiers such as passport or social security numbers, special categories of personal data (such as health, religion, sexual orientation or biometric data) at scale, or personal data of children.

Share links

  • Do not use share links to distribute content this policy forbids, to get around a suspension, or to publish material openly while presenting it as private.
  • Suspending an artifact stops its share links from opening and ends active link sessions. Reinstatement can make an unexpired, unrevoked link work again: suspension does not permanently revoke a link. Reports about a link are handled like reports about its artifact.

Branding

  • A brand on the Portifact bar may not use another company's or person's name or logo without the right to, Portifact's own name or logo, or a look-alike of either.
  • The link in a brand may not lead to phishing, malware or content this policy forbids.
  • Reports about a branded bar are handled like reports about the artifact, and we may suspend a brand without advance warning, followed by the explanation described under "What happens when the rules are broken".

Agents, tokens and automation

You are responsible for what your agents, service tokens and automation publish and do on Portifact with the access you give them, just as if you did it yourself. Review what they publish before you share it more widely. A workspace is responsible for what is done with its service tokens.

Features not yet available

When custom domains become available, these rules apply to them too: a custom domain may not impersonate another person, brand or Portifact itself, this policy applies to everything served on it, and abuse on a custom domain is reported like abuse on the artifact and ends with the domain's removal. Webhooks, integrations and audit streams may not be used to send requests to systems you are not allowed to use.

Reporting abuse

Report content that breaks this policy or the law by writing to abuse@portifact.com, or with the report form, which needs no account. Every artifact also has a report link at /__portifact/report on its own address, for example https://abcdefghij.portifact.dev/__portifact/report; Markdown and text artifacts show it as "Report this artifact" under the document, and the Portifact bar and the review viewer show it too.

For a full notice about illegal content, write to the email address and include the exact address of the content (the full link, including the page within the artifact), why you believe it is illegal or breaks this policy, your name and email address (unless the notice concerns child sexual abuse or exploitation offences for which the law lets you leave them out), and a statement that, to the best of your knowledge, the information is accurate and complete and that you send the notice in good faith. We acknowledge every notice, and tell you what we decided and how to ask for a review, without undue delay. We aim to respond within 48 hours. Please do not send us copies of illegal material; the address is enough. Copyright and trademark complaints follow the process in the Terms.

What happens when the rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may remove the content, suspend the artifact (which stops its share links) or a brand, limit features such as public sharing, or suspend or close the account. We can act without advance warning when people or the service are at risk, followed by the explanation described below. A person reviews each report and decides; rate limits, quotas and capacity protection are applied automatically and are not decisions about your content.

A suspended artifact is unavailable through its normal viewing address and shows the same notice as a missing one. The people who manage it see the reason in the dashboard and keep the export and deletion access described in the Terms, subject to any legally required preservation. When we restrict content, a feature or an account for illegality or a breach of this policy, we give the person affected a clear explanation no later than when the restriction takes effect, where we know how to contact them. It states what is restricted, its territorial scope and duration, the relevant facts, whether the decision followed a notice or our own review, any automated detection or decision-making used, and the legal or contractual ground and why it applies. It explains how to ask for our review and any available court or other redress. We withhold information only where the law permits. If you think we made a mistake, reply or write to abuse@portifact.com and we will review it.

We report suspected crimes that threaten someone's life or safety, and child sexual abuse or exploitation, to the competent authorities, and preserve the information the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We publish updates with their version and effective date. Materially adverse changes get at least 30 days' notice by email and on this page. The limited urgent security and legal exceptions in the Terms apply. Mandatory rights to refuse a change, end the contract and get a refund remain unaffected.
