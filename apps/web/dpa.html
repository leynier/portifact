This addendum is part of the Terms of Service. It sets the terms under which we process personal data for you when you decide how that data is used, or when you act for someone who does, as the data protection laws require. It applies automatically when we first process personal data on your behalf, and you do not need to sign it separately. The international transfer clauses have an additional requirement: before relying on them, we must complete and accept the private annex described below. Accepting the Terms does not complete that annex.

When this addendum applies

  • It applies between us and every customer for whom we process personal data on the customer's behalf: the customer that holds a workspace (the organization or person that asked for it and accepted the Terms for it), and the holder of a personal space who uses it for work. Roles follow the activity, not the kind of space.
  • It covers the personal data in the content and activity the customer directs: artifacts, comments, approval reviews, access logs, analytics counts, the audit log, groups, invitations, verified domains, policies and service tokens ("customer personal data").
  • It does not cover the processing we decide ourselves, as the Privacy Policy describes: account administration, sign-in, billing records, the security of the platform and the handling of abuse reports, also when those records concern a workspace.
  • "Data protection laws" means the laws that apply to the processing, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the GDPR as kept in United Kingdom law with the UK Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and United States state privacy laws. Words such as "controller", "processor", "personal data", "processing" and "personal data breach" have the meaning those laws give them.

Your contact and the private annex

  • Your contact is the data protection contact you give us. If you have not named one, we contact the workspace's admins or, for a personal space used for work, its account holder. We send breach notices, sub-processor notices and requests about customer personal data to that contact.
  • This addendum applies when we begin processing customer personal data. Before relying on its international transfer clauses, we and you complete a private written annex. It records both parties' legal names, addresses, countries, roles, activities and responsible contacts, including their names, positions and contact details, each party's electronic acceptance and its date, the UK Addendum's start date where it applies, and the name of the competent supervisory authority selected under Clause 13. Write to privacy@portifact.com to arrange it before the transfer begins.

Roles

You are the controller of customer personal data, or a processor acting for your own customer with its authorization, and we are your processor or sub-processor. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to process it, for telling the people it is about what you do with it before you invite, list or provision them (including that your admins and editors see every artifact of the workspace, every comment on them and who opened them, and your admins what each member did), and for the instructions you give us.

Processing on your instructions

  • We process customer personal data on your documented instructions. Your instructions are the Terms, this addendum, any written agreement for your plan, and what your admins, editors, members and service tokens do with the product's settings and features.
  • Any departure required by law must also be permitted by the data protection law and transfer safeguards that apply. For processing governed by Article 28 of the EU GDPR, the statutory exception concerns Union or Member State law; for the UK GDPR, it concerns applicable UK law. We assess foreign demands separately: a foreign authority's demand is not by itself a lawful basis for disclosure. We tell you before legally required processing unless the applicable law prohibits that notice.
  • We tell you at once if we believe an instruction breaks the data protection laws.
  • The subject, nature, purpose and duration of the processing, and the kinds of data and people, are in Annex I.

Confidentiality

The operator is the only person administering Portifact for us. Access is limited to what is needed to provide, secure and support the service. Authorized sub-processors and the people your access settings permit also process or access the relevant data. Anyone we authorize to process it in the future will be bound to confidentiality. If the law compels us to disclose customer personal data, we disclose only what is required and, where the law allows, tell you first.

Security

We take the technical and organizational measures described in Annex II to protect customer personal data, taking into account the state of the art, the cost, the nature of the processing and the risks to people. We may improve them over time, but never so that the overall protection is lower.

Sub-processors

  • You give us a general authorization to use sub-processors. The current list is in Annex III.
  • We give at least 14 days' notice, by updating the list on this page and emailing your contact, before a new or replacement sub-processor receives customer personal data. If an urgent security or continuity problem requires an earlier start, we explain the problem and obtain your specific written authorization before sending your data to that sub-processor.
  • If you object on reasonable data protection grounds, by writing to privacy@portifact.com, we resolve the objection, agree on an alternative, or end the affected service and refund its unused prepaid period before the proposed sub-processor starts processing your data.
  • Cloudflare processes the data under its published data processing addendum. Its terms do not reduce our obligations to you under this addendum. We remain responsible for the work of our sub-processors as the applicable contract and law require.

Helping you

  • Requests from people. If someone asks us to use their rights over customer personal data, we pass the request to your contact and do not answer it ourselves unless the law requires us to. The product provides tools according to each person's role: admins and editors can manage and export artifact data within their permissions, and admins manage the workspace's membership and settings and can read and export its audit data as the plan allows. Some requests need an admin or the operator. We help you carry out requests that the available tools do not cover.
  • Your other duties. We help you, with the information we have, with your duties on security, personal data breaches, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation with a supervisory authority.

Personal data breaches

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting customer personal data, we notify your contact without undue delay. We do not wait for a full investigation: we tell you what we know of its nature, the kinds and approximate number of people and records concerned, its likely consequences and what we are doing about it, and we add information as we learn it. You decide how to notify authorities and the people concerned, unless the law requires us to act directly. Telling you is not an admission of fault.

Return and deletion

A paid plan ending does not end the processing: while you keep the workspace or account, on the free plan or another plan, we keep providing the service. When you end the processing, by deleting the workspace or the account or by asking us to, we either return your customer personal data or delete it, as you choose. If you choose return, we also delete our remaining copies after completing the return. We keep only data that the law requires us to retain, for that purpose and period. We confirm completion in writing.

  • Return. Authorized users can export, at no charge, through the tools their role and plan allow, listed with their formats and limits in the export register: artifact files, versions, access logs, the audit log, each artifact's settings, access rules, share link settings, approval reviews, analytics, comments and comment action history, and, for a workspace's admins in the dashboard, its members, groups, invitations, email domains, policies, branding, service tokens and integrations, without secrets. Naming a data protection contact does not give that person product access. Your contact can ask us at privacy@portifact.com to arrange the return of data the tools or their permissions do not cover. When a paid plan ends, its listed retention limits continue for 60 days, but data keeps ageing under them, so export before the dates the dashboard shows. Where the EU Data Act applies, a switching request follows the Terms: we keep your paid plan in force at no extra charge through the transition and for 30 days after it, and because normal retention still applies to the live service, we separately preserve the exportable data needed for the switch before normal retention removes it and keep that export available to you for at least 30 calendar days after the transition ends.
  • Deletion. Deletion does not wait for an artifact's archive period. It blocks ordinary access to the affected artifacts, and background jobs remove their files, database records and access logs. The jobs retry unfinished work. The relay is scheduled every minute and maintenance every 15 minutes; these schedules do not guarantee a completion time, and we do not promise instantaneous removal from every underlying storage system. Database backup copies can remain for up to 30 days, outside ordinary use. Our restore procedure reapplies recorded deletion markers before access reopens, including those of deleted comments and threads, ended memberships, withdrawn invitations and email notices turned off. We keep markers of internal IDs, so those IDs are never reused. Lifecycle and moderation records (account and artifact IDs, actions, dates, reasons and operator notes) are deleted one year after the artifact or account they describe; comment-action records stay with the artifact and lose the author's ID when the author's account is deleted.

Workspaces are deleted by us at their admins' request.

Audits

We make available the information needed to show that we meet this addendum, including the security documentation, the source code of the service and written answers to your questions. We allow proportionate audits, including inspections where they are needed, by you or an independent auditor bound to confidentiality, at a time we agree. Routine audits happen at most once a year; that limit does not apply after a material incident, when there is credible evidence that we do not meet this addendum, or when a supervisory authority requires it. Reasonable security and scheduling arrangements will not be used to prevent an audit. Each side bears its own costs, unless the audit shows that we do not meet this addendum.

International transfers

We administer Portifact remotely from North America. The standard service has no contractual restriction on storage location. Our latest configuration check placed its default databases and artifact bucket in western North America, while other processing can occur across Cloudflare's global network, as Annex III explains. Customer personal data from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland may therefore be transferred outside them. Where a transfer needs a safeguard:

  • EU standard contractual clauses. The standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission in Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 are part of this addendum, and apply to a transfer only where it is within their scope, not to processing that the GDPR already governs directly. Module Two (controller to processor) applies where you are a controller, and Module Three (processor to processor) where you are a processor acting with your controller's authorization. You are the data exporter and we are the data importer. These choices apply: in Clause 7 the docking clause is included, and a new party joins only through the accession that clause describes; in Clause 9, option 2 (general written authorization) applies, with 14 days' notice; in Clause 11 the optional language is not used; in Clause 17, option 1 applies and the clauses are governed by the law of Ireland; in Clause 18(b), disputes go to the courts of Ireland. Annex I.A and I.B are Annex I below, Annex I.C is the supervisory authority of the data exporter determined under Clause 13, Annex II is Annex II and Annex III is Annex III.
  • United Kingdom. For transfers from the United Kingdom, the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses issued by the UK Information Commissioner (version B1.0, in force from 21 March 2022) is part of this addendum. Its Table 1 is completed by Annex I, Table 2 by the clauses and choices above, and Table 3 by the annexes of this addendum; in Table 4, both parties may end it as its Section 19 allows.
  • Switzerland. For transfers under the FADP, the EU clauses apply with these changes: the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner is the competent supervisory authority for transfers governed by the FADP; people who live in Switzerland can enforce their rights there, as the term "member state" includes Switzerland for that purpose; and references to the GDPR are read as references to the FADP for those transfers, without displacing the GDPR where both apply.
  • The clauses are completed by the private annex described under "Your contact and the private annex".

United States privacy laws

Where a United States state privacy law applies, we act as your service provider, contractor or processor, and:

  • we process the personal information only to provide Portifact and for the business purposes these documents describe, and not outside our direct business relationship with you or for any other commercial purpose;
  • we do not sell or share it, and we do not combine it with personal information from other sources, except as those laws allow;
  • we give it the protection those laws require, help you answer consumer requests, and tell you if we can no longer meet our obligations;
  • you may take reasonable steps to check that we use the data as these terms allow, and to stop and remedy any use they do not allow;
  • we bind our sub-processors to the same restrictions;
  • we certify that we understand these restrictions and will comply with them.

Data that needs special protection

Portifact is not designed for data that needs special legal protection: health records covered by laws such as HIPAA, payment card data, government identifiers, special categories of personal data at scale, or personal data of children. Do not send us such data without a written agreement with us.

Liability and order of precedence

No exclusion or limit of liability restricts liability or remedies under the applicable standard contractual clauses, including liability between their parties and the rights of the people the data is about, or what the data protection laws do not allow to be limited. The Terms' limits apply to other claims under this addendum where the law allows. If our documents conflict, they apply in this order: first mandatory law and the standard contractual clauses with the UK Addendum and the Swiss terms; then this addendum, for personal data; then a written agreement signed by both of us, for commercial matters; then the Terms. This addendum lasts as long as we process customer personal data for you.

Annex II: Security measures

These are the measures Portifact itself takes. Cloudflare's own measures and certifications are separate, and described in its data processing addendum.

  • Separation. Accounts, the API and the dashboard are served on portifact.com, and artifacts from their own addresses on portifact.dev, so a published page cannot act on an account. Security cookies are restricted to their host. Theme and language cookies are shared across Portifact's main and app domains, as listed in the Privacy Policy.
  • Access control. Every request is authorized by the same rules, whatever the interface (dashboard, CLI, API or MCP), by role and by the scopes the person approved. An artifact is served only to the people its access settings allow. Workspace policies can restrict access further.
  • Credentials. Sign-in codes, app and service tokens, share link tokens and passwords are stored only as hashes. Credentials that must be used again, such as signing secrets and connection credentials, are stored encrypted. Browser session identifiers and short-lived device login codes are stored as issued, in the access-controlled database. None of them is logged.
  • Transport. Connections to Portifact's web service and API use HTTPS, with strict transport security on Portifact's own domains.
  • Abuse protection. Rate limits, a human check for new accounts and new commenters, and limits on traffic and capacity.
  • Logging. Technical application logs contain IDs, counts and error labels, not content, comment text, email addresses, URL paths or credentials. Access and audit logs are separate records and contain the information listed in the Privacy Policy.
  • Resilience. Databases can be restored to any point of the last 30 days. Before access reopens, our restore procedure reapplies recorded deletion markers, current access restrictions and recorded deletion requests, as described under "Return and deletion".
  • Development. The source code is public. Every change is reviewed and tested before release, with automated tests, coverage and mutation thresholds and a secret scan.
  • Minimization. We collect only what the service needs, keep it as long as the Privacy Policy's retention table says, and never use content to train AI models.
