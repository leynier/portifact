What Portifact provides

Portifact publishes static artifacts (HTML sites, Markdown and plain text) at their own address, lets you decide who can open each one, and, as it becomes available, lets the people you invite comment on them. It runs no builds, servers or databases for your content.

  • Portifact is free. Free accounts have quotas, which today are 50 active artifacts, 250 MB per artifact, 2.5 GB per account, 100 publications every 24 hours, and 1 million requests and 25 GB served per calendar month across all of your artifacts. When the monthly traffic is used up, your artifacts answer with an error until the next month, while you can still publish and manage them. We may change the quotas; when we lower one, we will tell you first, and what is already published stays online until it expires.
  • Portifact is in early development. Features may change, move or be removed, and some described in the documentation are still being built. There is no guaranteed availability or support response time.
  • New accounts cannot make artifacts public during their first 7 days.

How long artifacts stay online

  • An artifact stays online for 30 days after you publish it. Every update or renewal starts the 30 days again.
  • After that it is archived: it goes offline but is kept for six months, and you can restore it at any time during that period.
  • After six months in the archive, it is deleted for good. You can also delete an artifact yourself at any time, which cannot be undone.

Portifact is not a backup service. Keep your own copies of anything you need.
